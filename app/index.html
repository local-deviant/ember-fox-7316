How the verdicts work

Every card carries a verdict, so a big percentage is never the whole story:

TRUE LOW
A source reports the lowest price on record for this product.
STRONG DEAL
At least 40% off, or at least $150 saved.
GOOD DEAL
A real saving, 20–39% off.
ROUTINE SALE
Under 20% off. A sale, not a steal — the card says so.

Discounts are computed from the two prices on the card, so the percentage always matches the prices you can check.

How these deals were checked

On 27 Nov 2026 I read each price at the store itself or in a deals roundup that names the store — RedmondPie's live Black Friday list, 9to5Toys, MacObserver, AppleInsider, CNN Underscored, Yahoo shopping, TechRadar, HotHardware and DealNews, plus the Best Buy product pages for the doorbusters. Each card names its source and the day it was read.

What I did not do: list a product, price or link no store or roundup showed, or inflate a discount with a made-up usual price. Where two roundups disagreed on a usual price (the Nintendo Switch 2 bundle), the card says so.

Prices and stock change fast on Black Friday. A deal can sell out or move between the check and your click — the store link on each card is the final word.
